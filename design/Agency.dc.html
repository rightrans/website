SERVICES · U.S. SALES AGENCY

Your sales team in the U.S., for manufacturers in China.

We act as your authorized U.S. agent: we find buyers, make sure your product meets U.S. rules, and stay on as the local contact after the sale.

WHO IT'S FOR

Built for manufacturers who are ready to export

Manufacturers with export experience

You already ship to other markets and have capacity for U.S. orders.

Brands entering the U.S.

You want a local partner rather than building a U.S. team on day one.

Factories hit by channel changes

You need new U.S. buyers and a compliant way to reach them.

WHAT WE DO

Everything between your factory and a U.S. purchase order

Market readiness review

Price benchmarks, required certifications and labels, tariff exposure (with a licensed customs broker) and the right channels. A clear go / no-go.

Sales representation

Target buyer lists, meetings in New York and beyond, trade shows, samples and negotiation of first orders, always with your approval.

Compliance coordination

Testing with accredited labs, U.S. labels and manuals, a buyer-ready document pack and supply-chain traceability files.

Localization and after-sales

English catalogs and spec sheets, quotes in U.S. dollars, and local handling of warranty and spare-parts requests.
