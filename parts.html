HOW WE WORK

Four steps from part number to supply.

1

Send part details

Part number, drawings, photos or the original part, and the equipment it goes in.

2

Feasibility and quote

We assess whether it can be replaced and quote the sample cost and time.

3

Test the sample

You test the sample on your own equipment and approve it.

4

Volume production

We produce to the approved sample.

How we're paid

Quoted in stages: an assessment first, then sampling and tooling quoted separately, and volume pricing only after you approve the sample. You can stop after any step.

Please note

  • —Every replacement must be tested and approved on your equipment.
  • —Safety-critical parts require approval from your engineering team.
  • —Replacements are compatible parts, not OEM parts.
