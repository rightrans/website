SERVICES · INTERNATIONAL SALES REPRESENTATION

Your products, in front of the right overseas buyers.

From New York, we represent your factory to distributors, retailers and corporate buyers in international markets.

WHO IT'S FOR

For manufacturers ready to sell overseas

Manufacturers entering new markets

Your product is proven, and you want to sell it abroad.

Exporters without an overseas sales team

You have exported before, but have no salespeople of your own abroad.

Not ready to open an overseas office

You don't want to set up a company or hire staff abroad from day one.

WHAT WE DO

We find buyers and work the deals

Market assessment

We work out who your product suits in international markets.

Buyer development

We find and contact potential distributors, retailers and corporate buyers.

Communication and negotiation

We present your product in English, answer questions and move inquiries through to negotiation.

Honest feedback

We pass buyer feedback and requirements back to you as they are.

HOW WE WORK

Four steps from first call to first order.

1

Tell us about your product

Share your product information, certifications and export history. We assess which markets fit.

2

Sign a representation agreement

We agree on the products, territories and commission.

3

Find buyers

We contact buyers and report regularly on who we reached and what they said.

4

Close orders

Buyers contract with and pay you directly. We keep following up on repeat orders.

How we're paid

Mainly commission on completed orders. Any upfront fee is set out in the representation agreement in advance; commission is settled at the agreed rate after an order closes and payment is received. The rate depends on the product category and is also set out in the agreement.

What we don't do

We don't hold inventory, forward freight or file customs entries, and we never act as importer of record. You or the buyer arrange these, and we can help you work out the steps.

Good to know

  • —Your product must meet the certification and compliance requirements of each target market (for example UL, FCC or FDA registration in the U.S., and CE marking in the EU, depending on the product).
  • —Overseas buyers often take months to decide. Plan for patient follow-up.
  • —Fees, shipping terms and other details are confirmed in the written agreement we both sign.
