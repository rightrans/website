Privacy policy

Who we are

Rightrans Inc. is a company based in New York, United States. If you have any question about this policy, email us at info@rightrans.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us through the contact form or by email: your name, company, email address, phone number or messaging ID, the service you are interested in, the details of your request and any files you attach.
  • Our website host may record basic technical data, such as IP addresses, for security. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare quotes.
  • To carry out work you ask us to do. We share project details with factories or buyers only as needed for your project, and you will know who they are.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not send you marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Service providers

We use a website host (GitHub Pages), an email provider (Zoho Mail) and a contact form service to receive your messages. They process information on our behalf, and it may be stored in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we handle your inquiry and any work that follows, and longer only where the law requires it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

Email info@rightrans.com to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have rights under data protection law, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal notice

About this website

Information on this website is general and is not an offer. The scope, fees and responsibilities for each project are set out in a written agreement.

Sales representation

We work to find buyers for the manufacturers we represent, but we do not guarantee sales results or order volumes.

Replacement parts

Replacement parts are compatible parts, not original (OEM) parts. Every replacement must be tested and approved by the customer on their own equipment, and safety-critical parts require approval from the customer's engineering team.

Shipping and import

Rightrans is not a freight forwarder, customs broker or importer of record.

Trademarks

Brand names, product names and part numbers on this website or in our quotes are used only to identify products. They belong to their respective owners.
